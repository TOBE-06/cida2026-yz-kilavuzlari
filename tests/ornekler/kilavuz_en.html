Our approach to generative AI

This page is a test fixture written for the CIDA 2026 pipeline. It imitates the structure of a newsroom policy on artificial intelligence and does not reproduce any real organisation's text.

Journalists must not publish text produced by generative AI without review by an editor. Any use of artificial intelligence in reporting should be disclosed to readers in a clear and visible way.

Editors are responsible for the accuracy of every story. AI-generated images will not be used to illustrate news events, and staff must check all facts against original sources before publication.

We will review these guidelines regularly as the technology and our understanding of it develop.
